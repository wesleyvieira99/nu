# Source notes / Notas de origem

This independent application study distinguishes public allegations, company replies, reported outcomes and original engineering proposals. It does not describe Nubank internal architecture or imply endorsement.

All 20 case sources were read in full through the browser. The source headers in `evidence/` are actual captured pixels, cropped for readability. `capture-register.json` records source URLs, crop coordinates and hashes. Raw page text and incidental personal content are not republished. Video links and credits distinguish journalism from official product tutorials.

Public reports do not establish incidence, technical root cause or current behavior. A resolved case remains resolved in the book. Reported amounts, customer liquidity, potential revenue, operational capacity and assumed investment costs are kept separate. Financial models are conditional, not measured returns.

The 20 Python labs are new for this evidence edition. The Clojure foundation core is retained and revalidated. All run locally with synthetic data. Production integration, policy approval and independent outcome measurement remain necessary.

Asset provenance and official technology mark sources are in `evidence/proveniencia.json` and `evidence/diagram-technology-map.json`. Marks identify proposed technologies, not confirmed internal components. Typeface licenses are retained in `fonts/`.
