# Source notes / Notas de origem

This is an independent engineering and leadership study prepared for a job application. It is not a Nubank product, internal architecture document or endorsed publication.

Este é um estudo independente de engenharia e liderança para candidatura. Não é um produto, documento de arquitetura interna ou publicação endossada pelo Nubank.

Public source links and page mappings are in `sources.json`. Image and logo provenance is in `evidence/proveniencia.json`. Screenshots preserve the original source language. Original public reports, individual experiences and official publications have different evidential limits, described in each case.

Marcas e fotografias mantêm seus respectivos titulares; as fontes e os créditos estão registrados no livro e nos manifestos. As fontes tipográficas incluem suas licenças OFL na pasta `fonts/`.

Edition V5 adds persistent chapter navigation and official technology marks within 24 distinct diagram compositions. The component-to-technology proposal and mark sources are recorded in `evidence/diagram-technology-map.json`. Chapter navigation and diagram geometry checks are included in `validation/`.

Diagrams are original external proposals. Illustrative snippets in `snippets/` do not all form standalone applications; the executable Python and Clojure labs are in `labs/`. None connects to a bank account or production banking service.
